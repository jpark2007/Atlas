Support

Something broken? Tell us.

If you can open Atlas

Use Report a Bug inside the app — on Mac it’s in Settings → App & Help, on iPhone in Settings → App & Help. It’s the fastest route: the report arrives with your app version and a tail of recent activity attached, which usually tells us what went wrong without a back-and-forth.

If you can’t

Use the form below. It reaches exactly the same place.

Common questions

My calendar isn’t syncing.

Open Settings → Calendars. Every source carries a live status dot — a stalled or errored connection says so there, and Reconnect on that account’s row fixes most cases (a Google grant can expire if the password changed or access was revoked).

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account, on either Mac or iPhone. It erases the account and everything in it — spaces, projects, tasks, events and notes — and it can’t be undone. If you’ve already removed the app and can’t get in, send the form above and we’ll do it for you.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No, and no. The privacy policy spells out exactly what’s stored and who can reach it.

How long until I hear back?

Atlas is built by two people, so replies come from a human and usually within a couple of days. Reports without an email address still get read — we just can’t write back.
